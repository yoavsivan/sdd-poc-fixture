F1' used conductor-new-track, then conductor-implement, then conductor-review — the same stock change flow the arm documents for a follow-up feature after setup is complete. conductor-new-track is prescribed because the project already has conductor/index.md and a tracks registry; a new track (spec.md + plan.md) is how Conductor records a distinct unit of work rather than amending the closed F1 track. Implementation then executes that plan under workflow.md TDD, and review is the stock close-out after the track is marked complete.
